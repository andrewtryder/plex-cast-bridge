    Speakers

    Each Cast destination can allow local playback and optionally advertise its own virtual Plex device in Google Home (for example Kitchen Plex). New speakers stay hidden from Google Home until you opt in. Room assignment for virtual devices still happens in the Google Home app.

    Stable IDs survive renames and IP changes. Forgetting a speaker removes its virtual Plex device on the next Google Home sync — it does not remove the Nest speaker from Google Home.
