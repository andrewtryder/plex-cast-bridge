# Optional local music index

The music index is disabled by default. Existing Smart DJ playback does not depend on it. In Settings → Library, enable indexing and choose manual, daily, or weekly scans. Indexing reads Plex metadata in pages of 100 and stores the resulting snapshot in `DATA_DIR/music_index.sqlite3` (normally `/data/music_index.sqlite3` in Docker). Scans do not play music or change Plex playlists.

An initial scan reads the library's track inventory. Scheduled scans fetch Plex's current library revision and track count first. If both match the last completed scan, the full enumeration is skipped; an unchanged library still gets a full reconciliation at least weekly to catch removals. If Plex does not provide a revision, the scanner falls back to a full scan. Changed inventories are read in pages of 100 and only changed track records are written. An active index remains available while a replacement scan is built; cancellation, failure, a changing library, or an interrupted restart leaves the last completed snapshot in place. Prevent overlapping scans and preserve the `data/` directory across container replacements. The index may reveal listening-library metadata; protect and back it up accordingly. Play counts reflect the last completed index and are refreshed on a full scan.

The status page reports indexed track, artist, and album counts and the availability of artist/album names, years, genre tags, mood tags, and Plex play counts. Missing metadata remains missing. Different Plex server and library identifiers are kept separate. No Plex tokens, audio files, or server addresses are stored in the index.

## Discover and save mixes

The DJ page can preview artist, decade, genre, mood, and deep-cut mixes from indexed metadata. Deep cuts sort tracks with known Plex play counts from least played upward; tracks without counts are excluded. Previewing does not start playback. You can reorder or remove tracks, play the preview on a selected speaker, and save, rename, edit, or delete a bridge-managed mix. Saved mixes are stored in SQLite and do not create or modify Plex playlists. If an indexed track disappears, it is marked unavailable and skipped during playback. Missing genre or mood tags can yield no results.

**AI-assisted mix generation is off by default.** When enabled in Settings → Library, selecting it for a preview sends a maximum of 50 locally selected tracks' limited metadata to Gemini. Indexing alone never calls Gemini. The free-form request, Plex credentials, server address, and audio are not sent. Returned IDs must all match the candidates. Metadata-only generation remains available without AI.
