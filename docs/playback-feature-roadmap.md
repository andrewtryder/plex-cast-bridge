# Playback feature roadmap

Research snapshot: 28 November 2026. Scope: improve the existing bridge and Default Media Receiver (DMR). This is a plan, not a promise that a source interface works with every Plex server, account, media file, or Cast device. No playback or server mutation was performed for this research. Preserve existing queue ownership, generation, device authorization, and cold-start safeguards in every stage.

## Feature assessments

Each item names the existing bridge surface, verified interface or upstream evidence, a deliverable, risks, and acceptance. Effort is relative (S: isolated change; M: multiple components; L: stateful or integration-heavy).

### 1. Native Plex playlists — missing; M

HA `plex/media_browser.py` enumerates audio playlists and browses their children. Bridge `playback_routes.py` browses artists/albums/tracks and `/api/library/play` expands artist or album; it does not handle playlist items as an ordered playlist. PlexAPI `PlexServer.playlists(playlistType=..., sectionId=..., ...)` and `Playlist.items(libtype=None)` exist, but the latter returns a materialized list. Browse with stable playlist IDs and play ordered track IDs through `SpeakerQueue.replace`, supporting smart, empty, deleted and mixed-type playlists; bound materialization and reject unsupported items. Preserve duplicates and ordering. Reuse artwork proxy and queue API. Tests: mocked server IDs, smart/deleted/empty/mixed playlist, duplicate tracks, bounded large playlist, concurrent replace and cross-library authorization. Hardware: audible playlist order, transitions, queue controls, and restart behavior. Do not claim `items()` provides pagination.

### 2. Favorites and recent discovery — partial; M

`music_index.py` and `music_index_scan.py` already persist library metadata and power saved mixes; they are opt-in derived data, not authoritative account history. HA `plex/sensor.py` uses `recentlyAdded(maxresults=1, libtype=...)`. PlexAPI `MusicSection.recentlyAdded`, `LibrarySection.search`, and server `history` exist; rating filters and account scope need validation for the actual server. Add separately labeled recently added, recently played, and favorites views with source, account scope, freshness and explicit empty/unavailable states; avoid treating last viewed as proof of audible play. Tests: fake account scope, rating values, no metadata, stale cache, pagination, deletion and server errors. Hardware: mark favorite and play a track through the bridge, then independently compare server history. Access/account rights and Plex version support are open questions.

### 3. Native Plex radio — partial; M/L

`plex_resolution.resolve_artist_radio` builds a bridge selection from artist/peer tracks and tags; it is not a Plex native station. `ai_router.py` supports Gemini selections and the bridge also has deterministic mixes. PlexAPI `MusicSection.stations()` returns a hub's station list or `None`, and `Artist.station()` returns a playlist or `None`; source does not establish entitlement, station expansion or ongoing refill. Offer native stations only when the server provides them, with a bounded initial queue and explicit failure; never silently substitute unrelated music. Tests: no hub, entitlement/error, empty station, duplicates, refill limits, exact artist IDs and race with stop. Hardware: authorized and unavailable accounts, successive tracks, long station and server/library changes. Avoid creating a `PlayQueue` unless its lifecycle and paging are independently validated.

### 4. Position, timestamp and seek — partial; M

`SpeakerQueue.status()` emits `position_ms` from Cast `current_time` and duration, but no sample timestamp; dashboard `static/dashboard/app.js` displays state. HA `plex/models.py` stores position and update timestamp; Cast `media_player.py` delegates seek. PyChromecast `MediaStatus.last_updated`, `adjusted_current_time`, `supports_seek`, and `MediaController.seek(position, timeout=10)` exist, with seconds as the Cast unit. The installed `seek()` sends `PLAYBACK_START`, so paused-session seek may resume playback and needs explicit design and verification. Return sampled position, UTC sample time, state, duration and seek capability together; interpolate only while playing, clamp to duration and expire stale or disconnected samples. Route seek through existing ownership and generation checks; do not seek external media. Tests: pause/buffer/unknown, stale clocks, reconnect, clamp, units and races. Hardware: compare audible position and dashboard across seek, pause, buffering, transitions and group playback.

### 5. Queue checkpoint and restart reconciliation — missing; L

`SpeakerQueue` owns in-memory current/upcoming/history, lock, generation, expected content and active session identity; tests in `test_local_playback.py` cover stale callbacks. Persist a versioned, atomic per-verified-speaker checkpoint containing stable Plex server/library/rating keys and queue order, never tokenized URLs. On restart resolve IDs and query fresh receiver status without launching an app. Resume ownership only with independent verified speaker identity and matching session/content; preserve external or indeterminate sessions. Offer explicit restore of a dormant queue rather than auto-load. Tests: crash during write, corruption, migration, stale/deleted IDs, different server, concurrent replacements, aliases, mismatched receiver and restart during load. Hardware: restart during active/paused bridge and external playback, verify no interruption or duplicate transitions. Receiver queues may outlive the process; session identity and URL expiry make implicit takeover unsafe.

### 6. Plex history and sessions — unverified/missing reporting; L

Bridge DMR plays Plex part URLs (`speaker_queue.py`); source alone does not establish correct Plex history or active sessions. Jellyfin reports start/progress/stop with session identity in `jellyfinActions.ts`; this is a design pattern, not a Plex protocol specification. PlexAPI `Playable.updateTimeline(time, state, duration)` exists and documents milliseconds but describes video; server `history` and `sessions` are observable separately. Do not emit timeline calls until supported audio semantics, account attribution, end thresholds and interaction with Plex's own tracking are established. Tests: fake monotonic progress, pause/seek/stop, identity changes, retry/dedupe, no report for external media, secret redaction. Hardware: compare actual server session/history for complete, skipped, paused, restarted and transcoded tracks; check for duplicate scrobbles. Any claim that DMR already records correct history remains a hypothesis.

### 7. Format compatibility and bounded fallback — partial direct play; L

`SpeakerQueue` loads a Plex part URL and content type, and handles load failures/watchdogs. Jellyfin constructs device profiles using receiver capability checks (`codecSupportHelper.ts`, `deviceprofileBuilder.ts`); a DMR sender cannot assume access to the same receiver-side capability APIs. PlexAPI `Playable.getStreamURL(**kwargs)` constructs a universal transcode URL for tracks; its existence does not prove codec, entitlement, session or seek behavior. Classify reliable unsupported-format failure separately from network/auth failure; permit at most one explicitly configured fallback per track/generation, with resource/time limits, secure URL handling and no retry on ownership loss. Tests: unsupported vs transient, fallback once, failure loops, stale callback, media types, token-free logs. Hardware: MP3/AAC/FLAC and other library formats on display, audio-only Nest and groups, inspect Plex direct play/transcode sessions, seek, pause and transition latency.

### 8. Lifecycle instrumentation — partial; M

`SpeakerQueue` has `load_media_failed`, load watchdog, session/content matching and queue generation; `activity.py` records recent events. Jellyfin's `maincontroller.ts` distinguishes media lifecycle and reports progress; do not infer that every CAF event exists in DMR sender callbacks. Add bounded, structured, secret-free events for request, selection, load, accepted/playing, buffering, finish, failure, timeout and ownership loss, with opaque correlation and monotonic durations. Distinguish confirmed receiver errors from timeout/unknown; do not infer audible playback from PLAYING. Tests: stale callback, duplicate completion, failure classification, watchdog, bounded retention and redaction. Hardware: record request-to-playing and independently observed audible time, transitions, failure and disconnect.

### 9. Runtime diagnostics — partial; M

`diagnostics_routes.py` reports linked Plex, last discovery, configuration-based Pub/Sub enabled status, cloud setup and sync. A flag or nonempty cached discovery is not evidence of current connectivity. `pubsub_worker.py` already tracks delivery outcomes and local dead-letter behavior. Separate configured, connected, stale, unreachable and unknown, with timestamped evidence and bounded read-only checks. Reuse existing status/health; avoid receiver launch, provider secrets, or consuming Pub/Sub messages. HA Cast `diagnostics.py` and maintained `discovery.py` offer structural examples. Tests: configured but disconnected, stale discovery, reconnect, disabled worker, no network, timeouts, no Cast launch. Hardware: compare dashboard indications to unplugged/reconnected Cast and Plex, plus a controlled voice delivery, without changing playback.

### 10. Sanitized issue export — missing; M

The existing backup route exports user data and credentials and is not an issue report. Create a separate previewable, opt-in allowlist export of software versions, state categories, timestamps and bounded sanitized events; omit tokens, URLs, IPs, project/subscription IDs, library names, media titles, device identifiers and message payloads by default. Treat exception strings, filenames and nested objects as untrusted; avoid relying only on regex redaction. Tests: seeded secrets in every field, malformed payloads, archive traversal, size caps and no backup content. Hardware: no playback needed; inspect a user-approved sample for useful evidence and private data before sharing.

### 11. Google Home MediaState — partial/conservative; L

`gcp-cloud-run/src/cloud_gateway/smart_home.py` advertises MediaState but `willReportState` is false and QUERY intentionally avoids invented playback state. HA `google_assistant/trait.py` maps entity states to trait data; HA has a live state store that our async Pub/Sub direction does not automatically provide. A truthful feature requires authenticated, bounded local-to-cloud state updates, stable device IDs, freshness/expiry, disconnect semantics and correct QUERY/Report State contracts; pending acknowledgements do not prove Cast success. Never publish default PLAYING or expose metadata unnecessarily. Tests: late/out-of-order updates, external app, offline, unknown, multiple speakers, auth and state expiry. Hardware: independently compare Home app QUERY/state with actual pause/play/buffering/disconnect; Google routing and trait behavior must be validated separately.

### 12. Efficient browsing and cache — partial; M

`/api/library/tracks` caps results, whereas `/api/library/browse` requests `offset + limit` and slices client-side, so deep pages get progressively more expensive. `music_index_scan.py` already uses `container_start` and page limits and preserves old snapshots after failed scans. Use server-side pagination with a stable sort and bounded page size, cache metadata by server/library/ID with TTL and revision invalidation, and preserve an explicit loading/error state. PlexAPI 4.18.3 explicitly declares `LibrarySection.search(..., maxresults, container_start, container_size, ...)`; `fetchItems` sends Plex container start/size headers. Confirm ordering and mutation behavior against the actual server. Tests: large offset request bounds, duplicate sort keys, cache invalidation, library switching, partial failure and missing art. Hardware: large library page latency and selection while server updates; no playback needed for browse checks.

### 13. Exact selection and ambiguity — partial; M

`plex_resolution.py` exact-filters album and track title but chooses the first album or track match; `speaker_routes.py` search and other paths also need review. The artist cold-start path rejects multiple exact names; `cast_manager.py` rejects ambiguous speaker candidates. The direct library path uses `fetchItem(rating_key)`, while mixes scope IDs to server/library. Centralize selection around verified stable server/library/item identities; reject or present candidates for duplicate titles/artists/editions rather than selecting first. Validate resolved item type and library for dashboard, queue, Smart DJ, radio and voice before Cast side effects. Tests: identical names across artists/albums/libraries, Unicode/case, invalid/deleted ID, duplicate device names, stale/malicious IDs and no side effect on ambiguity. Hardware: choose between two real same-title tracks and confirm displayed and audible selection. Voice must not pretend Google routes arbitrary artist requests to this integration.

## Delivery sequence

1. **Selection correctness and browse paging** (13, 12): independent API changes and visible ambiguity handling. Preserve identity/security checks; measure deep-page requests.
2. **Playlists and discovery views** (1, 2): deliver native playlists separately from recent/favorites; show permissions and empty/error states.
3. **Position and seek** (4): add timestamp and capability first, then owned-session seek; do not require persistent queue.
4. **Diagnostics and lifecycle** (8, 9, 10): ship read-only evidence and previewable sanitized export before richer reporting; keep backups separate.
5. **Native radio** (3): prototype server capabilities and entitlements before exposing a bounded selection; keep bridge radio available under its existing name.
6. **Recovery** (5): explicit queue restore after ownership reconciliation, with no automatic takeover.
7. **Format fallback and history** (7, 6): separate experiments and releases after observed Plex/receiver behavior; avoid retry or scrobble claims without evidence.
8. **Google Home MediaState** (11): requires its own authenticated state architecture, freshness design and Google acceptance; do not gate earlier dashboard improvements on it.

## Existing tests and acceptance gates

`tests/test_plex_resolve.py` exercises artist, album, track and bridge radio resolution; add duplicate-match cases instead of assuming its current first-match tests prove uniqueness. `tests/test_music_index.py` covers snapshot failure, library scope, index migrations and mixes. `tests/test_local_playback.py` and `tests/test_queue_and_artwork.py` cover queue transitions, stale load callbacks, ownership-related races and volume. `tests/test_pubsub_callback_integration.py` exercises dispatch, acknowledgement, device IDs and failures. `tests/test_voice_cold_start.py`, `tests/test_cast_group_state.py` and `tests/test_identity_routing.py` protect cold-start and identity. These establish unit behavior, not real Plex session reporting or device codec compatibility.

Before candidate hardware acceptance, follow [Voice Cold Start acceptance](voice-cold-start-acceptance.md), including physical active/paused static and dynamic group cases, overlaps, disconnected controllers and group changes during selection. Also measure sender disconnect, bridge restart and queue continuity, Plex sessions/history, request-to-audible and track-transition latency, direct play/transcoding by format, position accuracy, and audio-only/group behavior. Treat an inactive media namespace as indeterminate until separately supported by evidence. Independent senders can change state between final check and load; document the residual race rather than claiming atomic exclusion.
